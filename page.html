Texas.Gov Payments General Maintenance

Notice to Operators: RRC Online Payments will be intermittently unavailable October 29, 2026, from 10:00 PM to 11:00 PM CT.  During this time, customers using RRC applications will be unable to process online payments through Texas.gov payment services and will be redirected to a maintenance page and unable to complete payments until after the maintenance is complete.

The proposed amendments to rules §§ 3.15 and 3.58, related to Senate Bill 1150 (89th Legislative Session, 2025), have been posted for public comment. Comments will be accepted until 5:00 pm on Friday, November 13, 2026. For more information, including an online comment form, please visit the RRC Proposed Rules page.

Gas Masters

The Gas Masters data sets contain the most recent 12 months of production and 14 months of allowable data for each gas well in the state of Texas. Also included is:

  • general oil and gas information pertaining to the individual fields;
  • field allocation formulas and their corresponding factors;
  • general gas information pertaining to the individual wells, gatherers, and balancing status;
  • general information pertaining to individually completed wells and multiple completions;
  • well allowable
  • and well production information.
Back

Oil Masters

The Oil Masters data sets contain the most recent 12 months of production and 14 months of allowable data for each oil lease in the state of Texas. Also included is general information pertaining to:

  • individual fields, field allocation formulas and their corresponding factors;
  • individual leases, gatherers, balancing status, allowable and production information;
  • individually completed wells and multiple completions, well testing information, allowable and production information.
Back

PR (P1/P2) Gas Disposition

These data sets contain gas disposition information extracted from the Form PR (Production Report) during a given monthly production cycle. It includes the most recent reporting month and corrected reports for the previous production months.

Back

Historical Ledger

The Historical Ledger database is an online disk resident file that contains information related to lease level oil and gas production, product dispositions, and allowable information. The Historical Ledger provides monthly oil and gas production by lease for the period January 1993 to 27 months prior to the present date. The data is incremented monthly as the Oil and Gas Roll process "rolls off" selected data from the 26-month Oil and Gas systems such as the Production database above. The Historical Ledger database is updated daily because of Monthly Production Report (Form PR) received relating to the Historical Ledger time period.

Production Report for Pending Leases

This data sets contains a dump of the Production Reports for Pending Leases Database. When a lease has not yet been assigned an official Lease ID by the RRC, the operator will report production on this “pending lease” by using some other identifying information [API (American Petroleum Institute) number or drilling permit number). Data for pending wells provided on or after September 1, 2016 will no longer include duplicate records not deleted after the assignment of an oil lease or gas well identification number.

R3 Gas Processing Plants

This data set includes the data submitted in Form R-3 [Monthly Report for Gas Processing Plants] Reports accepted by the RRC in the month identified in the file’s name. Please note that information submitted in sections VIII and IX on the form are not available in the data set.

Back

Full Wellbore Wellbore

This database contains information from the Wellbore database. Every proposed hole in Texas relating to oil and gas drilling has an API (American Petroleum Institute) number assigned to it. Records for each hole drilled are set up in the Wellbore database by API (American Petroleum Institute) number. These records contain completion, plugging, formation, and other information related to the well bore. Dummy API numbers have been assigned to old wells drilled prior to implementation of the API numbering technique. Information on most of the wells ever drilled in Texas is contained in the Full Well Bore file.

Back

Wellbore Query Data

This data set provides data related to oil and gas well bores used in the RRC Online Oil & Gas Data Well Bore Query. The Well Bore Query data set provides a way for users to search for wells by district, lease, county, field, operator, drilling permit number, API number, on/off schedule, or well type. The file is run at the beginning of each month.

Back

Statewide Oil and Gas Well Data

The primary file of the automated well system is the Well Database. It is an on-line, disk resident file that contains testing and technical information related to oil and gas wells. The file information is made available in two volumes, the oil wells or the gas wells. The Well Database contains allowable, G-10 and W-10 testing/technical information on a well basis for the most recent 26 month reporting period.

Back

Oil Detail Well

This data set supports the multi-well records on the Oil Masters tapes. It contains each detail well and any pertinent information concerning that well, including:

  • general oil information pertaining to the individual well,
  • W-10 test data,
  • 16 Texas Administrative Code 14(B)(2) [Statewide Rule 14(B)(2)] data, and
  • shut-in information.
Back

Oil Well Status (26 Month W-10)

This data set contains Oil Well Status Report (Form W-10) test information, including reports filed late, for the most recent 26 month reporting period. The Form W-10 must be filed annually or semi-annually for every oil well in Texas and RRC rules determine which month the status report/test must be filed.

Back

Gas Well Status (26 Month G-10)

This data set contains Gas Well Status Report (Form G-10) test information, including reports filed late, for the most recent 26 month reporting period, keyed during a month. The Form G-10 must be filed annually or semi-annually for every gas well in Texas and RRC rules determine which month the status report/test must be filed.

Back

Directional Survey Applications

Imaged Completion Files

This data set is a zipped file that will include images of all completion forms and attachments, organized by RRC district, submitted or approved the previous day. Completion images will be in .PDF and .TIFF file formats.

High Cost Gas

This data set contains information on every application for high cost gas severance tax incentive certification, which includes information on tight sands, deep gas, and other as specified by the operator. Gas from wells defined as high cost gas wells under Section 107 of the old Federal Natural Gas Policy Act (NGPA) may be eligible for a state severance tax reduction or exemption. Section 107 includes gas from tight sands, completions below 15,000 feet, Devonian shale, coal seams, or geopressured brine. Reference 16 Texas Administrative Code §101(c)(2) [Statewide Rule 101(c)(2)].

Back

Hight Cost Gas (Tight Sands Only)

This data set contains information on every application for high cost gas severance tax incentive certification for tight sands. Gas from wells defined as high cost gas wells under Section 107 of the old Federal Natural Gas Policy Act (NGPA) may be eligible for a state severance tax reduction or exemption. Section 107 includes gas from tight sands, completions below 15,000 feet, Devonian shale, coal seams, or geopressured brine. For applicability refer to Reference 16 Texas Administrative Code §101(c)(2) [Statewide Rule 101(c)(2)].

Back

Natural Gas Policy Act

This data set contains Natural Gas Policy Act (NGPA) Section 107 data from tight sands, completions below 15,000 feet, Devonian shale, coal seams, or geopressured brine, including:

  • operator name and address;
  • well number, lease or well ID number, and district;
  • docket number; and
  • date of first production for the high-cost gas incentive program.
Back

ST-1 Application Report

This data report provides high cost gas certification data of gas leases from the Application for Texas Severance Tax Incentive Certification (ST-1) online application submitted since April 28, 2019

Underground Injection Control Database

The Underground Injection Control (UIC) Well Inventory System was implemented in January 1980. This file contains information related to all underground injection wells in Texas, including inventory and permit specific data, H-10 monitoring data, H-5 pressure testing data, and UIC enforcement action data. In 1988, a monitoring system was established and enforcement information was added to the RRC automated files for this system.
